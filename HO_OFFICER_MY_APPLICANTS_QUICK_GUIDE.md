# HO Officer "My Applicants" - Quick Reference Guide

## 🎯 What Changed?

### For HO Recruitment Officers

**Sidebar Navigation**:
- ✅ **NEW**: "My Applicants" - Shows only YOUR assigned applicants
- ❌ **REMOVED**: "Officers" - No longer accessible
- ❌ **REMOVED**: "Applicants" - Redirects to "My Applicants"

**Dashboard Quick Menu**:
- ✅ **NEW**: "All Applicants" button - Click to view pending transfers (auto-redirects to My Applicants for security)

**Security Features**:
- 🔒 Agent names are **completely hidden**
- 🔒 Only see applicants **assigned to you**
- 🔒 Cannot create new applicants (assigned by Admin/President)
- 🔒 Cannot remove security filters

---

## 🚀 How to Access Your Applicants

### Method 1: Sidebar (Recommended)
1. Look at the left sidebar
2. Click **"My Applicants"**
3. View all your assigned applicants

### Method 2: Dashboard Widget
1. View "My Recent Assigned Applicants" on your dashboard
2. Click **"View all my assigned applicants →"** at the bottom
3. Opens the full list of your assigned applicants

### Method 3: Quick Menu
1. Click **"All Applicants"** in the Quick Menu on your dashboard
2. Automatically redirects to "My Applicants" (security feature)

---

## 📋 What You Can Do

### ✅ Available Actions
- View all applicants assigned to you
- Filter by branch, stage, status, date
- Search by applicant name or email
- View individual applicant profiles
- Verify documents for your assigned applicants
- Advance applicants through stages
- Approve/reject stage advancements

### ❌ Restricted Actions
- Cannot see applicants NOT assigned to you
- Cannot see agent names or information
- Cannot create new applicants
- Cannot remove the "assigned to me" filter
- Cannot access the generic "Applicants" page

## 🔐 Security Notices

### When You Try to Access "All Applicants"
**You'll see**:
```
🔒 HO Officer redirected from All Applicants to My Applicants
```
**What this means**: For security, you can only access your assigned applicants.

### When No Applicants Are Shown
**If you see "No applicants found"**:
- This is normal if no applicants have been assigned to you yet
- Contact Admin/President to request applicant assignments
- Assignments happen when Admin/President approves transfers to HO

---

## 📞 Getting Applicants Assigned

### How Assignment Works
1. **Branch Manager** requests transfer to HO (clicks "Advance to Next Stage" at Transfer stage)
2. **Admin/President** receives pending approval notification
3. **Admin/President** opens "Pending Stage Approvals"
4. **Admin/President** clicks "Approve" on the transfer request
5. **Admin/President** selects YOUR name from the "HO Recruitment Officer" dropdown
6. **Admin/President** clicks "Confirm Assignment"
7. ✅ **You now see the applicant** in "My Applicants"

### If You Need More Applicants
- Contact your Admin or President
- Request assignment of specific applicants
- Provide reason for the assignment request

## 🎓 Best Practices

### Daily Workflow
1. **Morning**: Check "My Applicants" for new assignments
2. **During Day**: Process documents and advance stages
3. **End of Day**: Update applicant statuses and notes
4. **Weekly**: Review all assigned applicants for progress

### Document Verification
1. Open applicant profile from "My Applicants"
2. Navigate to "Documents" tab
3. Review each document carefully
4. Click "Verify" or "Reject" with clear notes
5. Document status updates automatically

### Stage Advancement
1. Ensure all documents for current stage are verified
2. Click "Advance to Next Stage"
3. System checks if all requirements are met
4. If approved, applicant moves to next stage
5. If requires approval, Admin/President will be notified

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see any applicants"
**Solution**: No applicants have been assigned to you yet. Contact Admin/President.

### "All Applicants redirects to My Applicants"
**This is correct**: This is a security feature. You can only access your assigned applicants.

### "I can't see agent names"
**This is correct**: Agent information is hidden from HO officers for security.

### "I can't create new applicants"
**This is correct**: Only Branch Managers can create applicants. They are then assigned to you.
